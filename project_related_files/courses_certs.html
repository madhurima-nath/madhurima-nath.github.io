Energy and Climate Scenario Analysis, Cornell University, New York, USA Aug – Dec 2026

Syllabus: This course examines quantitative methods to support energy transition and inform climate-change mitigation policies. Students will learn about widely used software tools in the field, such as LEAP and NEMO, to assess energy transition pathways and policies. The course covers the analysis of energy demand and supply for different economic sectors as they navigate the shift toward low-carbon energy systems. Mathematical models will be utilized to identify optimal policies and energy transition scenarios. Finally, students will learn how to perform a cost-benefit analysis to quantify the effects of the identified scenarios.

Leave No Trace in Daily Life & Urban Connections Certifications · 2026

The Leave No Trace in Daily Life course focuses on extending the seven principles of Leave No Trace beyond trails and campsites into everyday choices — waste, energy use, transportation, and purchasing decisions — that add up at scale when practised consistently.
The Urban Connections course brings outdoor ethics education to communities in cities.

Climate Intelligence: Using Climate Data to Improve Business Decision-Making, University of Reading (FutureLearn) · 2026

Completed the Climate Intelligence certification, run by the University of Reading, UK. It covers ways to identify reliable climate and weather projection data, use it to assess business risk from extreme weather and climate variability, and apply forecast data — illustrated through sector case studies — to strategic decision-making.
